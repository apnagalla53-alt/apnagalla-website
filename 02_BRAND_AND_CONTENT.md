# Apna Galla — Brand & Content Guide

## Brand

Name:
**Apna Galla**

Domain:
**ApnaGalla.com**

Main tagline:
**Dukaan ka hisaab. Bas poochho.**

Secondary:
**Bill banao. Khata rakho. Stock sambhalo. Business se kuch bhi poochho.**

## Public Positioning

Do not lead with:
- ERP
- enterprise software
- accounting suite
- complex automation

Lead with:
- billing
- khata
- stock
- shop management
- business understanding
- Ask Apna Galla

## Tone

Use:
- simple Hindi/Hinglish
- short sentences
- familiar retail language
- clear benefits
- visual examples

Avoid:
- technical jargon
- fake superlatives
- fake customer counts
- fake certifications
- fake integrations

## Public Product Names

- Ask Apna Galla
- Apna Galla Insights
- Apna Galla Starter
- Apna Galla Smart
- Apna Galla Pro
- Apna Galla Backup
- Complete Business Export

## Visual Direction

- light neutral background
- white / soft cards
- quiet green accent
- dark readable text
- subtle borders
- generous negative space
- one coherent icon family
- mobile-first
- professional but not corporate

Avoid:
- copied competitor layouts
- excessive gradients
- glassmorphism
- dense admin-dashboard feel
- tiny typography

## Trust Copy

Recommended:
- “Your business records stay protected on every paid plan.”
- “Export options depend on your plan.”
- “Your shop data stays isolated from other businesses.”
- “Apna Galla never invents profit or business numbers.”

Do not claim:
- GST filing included
- government integration
- bank-grade/military-grade security
- guaranteed uptime
unless objectively true.
